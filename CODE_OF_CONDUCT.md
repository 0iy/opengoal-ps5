# Code of conduct

This is a small hobby project. Be kind and patient, assume good faith, and keep discussions about the work.

- Welcome newcomers and questions, also basic ones.
- Criticise code and ideas, not people. No harassment, insults or discrimination.
- Do not post private data (addresses, tokens, account names) of yourself or others.
- No piracy: do not ask for or share game files, ISOs, keys or firmware here. Issues and discussions that do are closed and removed.

The maintainer may edit, close or remove contributions that break these rules and may block repeat offenders.
Report a problem by opening a discussion or, for something private, through the contact route in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).
